Privacy policy

rclone (personal) · last updated 10 November 2026

Who uses it. This OAuth client is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computers, to access the owner's own Google Drive accounts.

What it accesses. With the owner's consent it reads, copies and lists files in those Google Drive accounts in order to copy them between accounts and verify the copies.

Where data goes. Files are copied directly between Google Drive accounts or to the owner's own computers. No data is sent to, stored by, sold to, or shared with any third party, and none is used for advertising or analytics.

Credentials. Access tokens are stored only in a private configuration file on the owner's computers. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API data. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
